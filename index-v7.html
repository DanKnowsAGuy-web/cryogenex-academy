Bill one

Electricity. Your worst fifteen minutes sets the whole month.

The demand charge is set by one moment: the hottest fifteen minutes of the month, when every rooftop unit on the roof runs flat out at once. You pay for that spike all month, and on many tariffs for a year after.

A fouled unit runs longer and hits that peak harder. Restore it and the peak comes down. On units past ten years, recovery has measured 20 to 24 percent. Solar thermal adds at least 30 percent more.

Electricity is 94 percent of Academy's measured footprint. Academy GHG report, 2021.

Bill two

Maintenance. Heat kills compressors.

Fouled coils run hot. Hot units fail, leak, and call for service in July.

Restore heat transfer and readings drop within 48 hours. We expect 15 to 25 percent fewer failures. The pilot's service log will confirm it.

Bill three

Replacement. $240,000 a roof. 322 roofs.

Twelve units a store, fifteen years each: about $77 million across the fleet, usually arriving as summer failures.

Units that run cooler last three to five years longer, moving replacement onto your build schedule.

Mechanism

Why one treatment moves all three.

Compressor oil bakes onto the coil as an insulating film, trapping heat. One treatment strips it and keeps the metal clean. No shutdown, no modification, warranty intact.
