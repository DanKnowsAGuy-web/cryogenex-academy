15min

Your worst fifteen minutes sets the whole month.

The demand charge is set by one moment: the hottest fifteen minutes, when every unit on the roof runs flat out at once. You pay for that spike all month.

A fouled unit runs longer and hits that peak harder. Restore it and the peak comes down. On units past ten years, recovery has measured 20 to 24 percent.1, 8

Electricity is 94 percent of Academy's measured footprint. Academy GHG report, 2021.

Heat kills compressors. Then it sends the bill.

Fouled coils run hot. Hot units fail, leak, and call for service in July. We expect 15 to 25 percent fewer failures, and the pilot's service log will confirm it.12

Replacement is the slow version: twelve units a store at $240,000 a roof, about $77 million across 322 stores. Units that run cooler last three to five years longer, moving replacement onto your build schedule.

Why one treatment moves all three.

Compressor oil bakes onto the inside of the coil as an insulating film, trapping heat. Strip the film and every bill drops at once.
