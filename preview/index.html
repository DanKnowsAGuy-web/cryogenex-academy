Questions

I run facilities. What changes for me? ▾

Nothing breaks and nothing changes hands. A day per store on the units you already run, no shutdown, warranty intact. Your service contractor stays your contractor. Fewer hot units means fewer July calls.12

I own the P&L. What does it cost? ▾

The review and the pilot are free. Rollout is a fixed multiple of first-year savings, with payback written down first, typically 12 to 24 months. Every rebate and incentive the work qualifies for is found and filed for you.1, 2, 4

I answer for ESG. How is it measured? ▾

Electricity is 94 percent of Academy’s measured footprint (2021 GHG report). Every pilot store is measured against untreated controls, witnessed by any third party you choose, under IPMVP on request. Verified kWh you can carry straight into reporting.8
